# Reserve Study & Long-Term Planning

A Reserve Study is the primary tool for ensuring the Building Fund remains solvent over the long term. Results feed directly into the annual budgeting process managed by the [[building-fund-roles|Deacon Board and Finance Department]].

## Study Cadence

| Study Type | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Professional reserve study | Every **5 years** |
| In-house annual update | Every **year** |

A professional study is preferred; an in-house study is an acceptable alternative when budget constraints make a professional study impractical.

## What the Study Must Include

1. A full inventory of building components (e.g., HVAC, roof, parking lot)
2. Assessment of remaining useful life and current replacement cost for each component
3. Recommendations for annual reserve contributions
4. A target **Fully Funded Balance** and **Percent Funded** benchmark

## 80% Funded Rule

The Fund must maintain a **Threshold Funding Level of at least 80% funded** at all times.

- **Percent Funded** = End Balance ÷ Fully Funded Balance (reflecting accrued depreciation)
- If the Fund falls below 80%, the Deacon Board must develop a **catch-up plan** to restore funding within a **5-year window**

The Deacon Board reviews Reserve Study results annually and incorporates recommendations into the budgeting process. See [[building-fund-reporting|Reporting]] for how reserve status is communicated to the congregation.
