# Building & Renovation Fund

The Building & Renovation Fund supports OIC Church's long-term stewardship of its physical property — covering major repairs, facility improvements, and emergency capital needs. It is a restricted fund, distinct from the [[handbook-finance|Finance]] operating budget, governed by the Deacon Board under Elder oversight.
