# Eligible Uses

The Building & Renovation Fund is designated **exclusively** for capital facility needs. See [[building-fund-thresholds|Approval Thresholds]] for the dollar limits that apply to each expenditure, and [[building-fund-roles|Roles & Governance]] for who authorizes spending.

## Covered Uses

1. **Deferred maintenance and major component repair/replacement** — e.g., roofing, HVAC, flooring, plumbing
2. **Facility improvements** to support growth, accessibility, or ministry expansion
3. **Emergency capital needs** — urgent and unplanned repairs that cannot wait for a normal budget cycle

## Excluded Uses

Routine maintenance and operational supplies are **not** covered by this Fund. Those expenditures must be budgeted separately in the **Operating Fund**.

> **Rule of thumb:** If it keeps the lights on day-to-day, it belongs in the Operating Fund. If it replaces or significantly extends a major building component, it belongs here.
