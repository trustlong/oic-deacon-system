# Approval Thresholds

All Building Fund expenditures must be approved according to the tier that matches the spend amount. Thresholds assume a **$250,000 annual budget**. See [[building-fund-roles|Roles & Governance]] for a description of each approving body.

## Approval Tiers

| Amount | Approval Required |
|---|---|
| **Under $1,250** | Operations Chair and Treasurer |
| **$1,250 – $12,500** | Full Deacon Board approval |
| **Over $12,500** | Congregational approval, upon recommendation of the Deacon Board and concurrence of the Elder Board |

## Check Control

All checks drawn on the Building Fund must be **signed by two authorized individuals**, and the checkbook keeper is explicitly excluded from being one of the two signatories. This dual-signature requirement applies regardless of dollar amount.

See [[building-fund-eligible-uses|Eligible Uses]] to confirm an expenditure qualifies before routing it for approval.
