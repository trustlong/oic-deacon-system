# Reporting & Transparency

Reporting is prepared by the Building Fund Subcommittee or the Finance Department. See [[building-fund-roles|Roles & Governance]] for who holds these responsibilities.

## Annual Building Fund Report

The report must include all of the following line items:

| Item | Description |
|---|---|
| Beginning balance | Fund balance at the start of the fiscal year |
| Contributions | All deposits received during the year |
| Interest earned | Investment or savings interest accrued |
| Expenses | All expenditures drawn from the Fund |
| Ending balance | Fund balance at close of the fiscal year |
| Reserve study status | Current Percent Funded and any catch-up plan status |

## Congregational Meeting

Fund status and project updates must be presented at **at least one congregational meeting per year**. This is the primary transparency checkpoint for the membership.

Fundraising campaigns linked to specific projects must clearly inform donors that contributions go to a **restricted fund under church leadership oversight** and are **not refundable**.

See [[building-fund-reserve-study|Reserve Study]] for how Percent Funded is calculated, and [[building-fund-thresholds|Approval Thresholds]] for the spending controls that govern how reported expenditures were authorized.
