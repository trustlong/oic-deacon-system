# Roles & Governance

The Building & Renovation Fund involves several overlapping roles. See [[building-fund-thresholds|Approval Thresholds]] for how these roles map to spend tiers, and [[building-fund-reporting|Reporting]] for each role's reporting obligations.

## Roles

### Deacon Board
- Holds primary **oversight and administration** of the Fund
- Approves expenditures in the $1,250–$12,500 tier
- Recommends projects over $12,500 to the congregation
- Reviews Reserve Study results annually and incorporates recommendations into budgeting
- May appoint a Building Fund Subcommittee to advise and execute responsibilities
- Recommends any Fund closure or restructuring plan, subject to Elder Board approval

### Elder Board
- Provides **operational oversight** to ensure Fund use aligns with OIC's mission and ministry needs
- Must **concur** on any expenditure requiring congregational approval (over $12,500)
- Reviews and is aware of the proposed annual Fund contribution during budget preparation
- Approves any Fund closure or restructuring plan recommended by the Deacon Board

### Building Fund Subcommittee *(optional)*
- Appointed by the Deacon Board as needed
- Advises on planning, implementation, and reporting
- May prepare the annual Building Fund Report and congregational updates

### Operations Chair & Treasurer
- **Co-approvers** for expenditures under $1,250
- Both serve as **authorized signatories** on Fund checks
- The checkbook keeper may not be one of the two required check signatories

### Finance Department
- Participates in annual budget preparation alongside the Deacon Board
- Reviews the Reserve Study's recommended annual contribution
- May prepare the annual [[building-fund-reporting|Building Fund Report]]

### Congregation
- Receives at least one annual update on Fund status at a congregational meeting
- Provides **final approval** for expenditures over $12,500, upon Deacon recommendation and Elder concurrence
- Donor contributions to the Fund are restricted and non-refundable; donor designations are honored when feasible but are non-binding on the board
