> **Goal:** redirect spiritual / non-tangible needs to elders cleanly — while keeping elder load as *low as possible* by resolving everything tangible within the diaconate.

## To design here

- The test for "tangible vs. spiritual" (see [[handbook-tangible-vs-spiritual]]).
- What gets redirected to elders vs. handled by deacons vs. handled jointly.
- The minimal-handoff pattern: what the deacon does before involving an elder (gather info, meet the tangible part, hand over only the spiritual part).
- The specific escalation triggers already in policy — e.g. Benevolence cases that *"materially reduce the fund"* go to elders ([[benevolence-roles]]); building spend over threshold needs elder concurrence ([[building-fund-thresholds]]).
- How to escalate to a *specific* member/resource first, before defaulting to an elder.

## Draft rule

> Default to resolving within the diaconate. Escalate to elders only when the need is **spiritual in nature**, requires **spiritual authority**, or hits a **policy threshold** that requires elder concurrence. When escalating, hand over a clear summary so the elder spends minimal time re-gathering.
