# On-Call Runbook

The half-asleep, phone-in-hand version. Full detail lives in [[deacon-playbook]] and [[duty-roster]] — this page is a distillation, not a replacement. If anything here ever conflicts with those, they win; fix this page.

## Am I on duty?

Check the **Duty Roster tab**. If you're not sure, assume you might be and act — a request never goes unhandled because of uncertainty about whose week it is.

> **Chair:** ______________________ (phone: ______________________)
> **This week's backup:** ______________________ (phone: ______________________)
>
> *(deacons: keep these two lines current every week — this is the one thing on this page that goes stale fastest)*

---

## A request just came in

Work top to bottom. Don't skip steps because they seem obvious in the moment.

1. **Log it** in the tracker. Not later — now. Status: **New**.
2. **Acknowledge the member.** Same day if it smells urgent; otherwise within 2-3 days. You're confirming a human heard them, not promising a fix.
3. **Intake personally** — a call or face-to-face, not a text thread. Get: what's needed, any deadline, what's been tried, whether there's a spiritual dimension.
4. **Self-help fit?** If yes, offer it — but never as a brush-off. If they're distressed or time is short, skip straight to routing.
5. **Triage:** tangible or spiritual? (Spiritual → elder, see below.) Which function? What urgency tier? (See tables below.)
6. **Pick ONE named owner** — Helpers Directory match → sub-lane co-worker → Associate Director → Director → Chair → Elder (only if spiritual/policy). Stop at the first real fit.
7. **Hand off person-to-person.** Call them. Get a verbal yes. Never a group text, never "the team will handle it."
8. **You're done.** Update tracker: owner, handoff date, status **Assigned**. Do not follow up — that's the owner's job now. [[crack-detection]] catches stalls; that goes to the Chair, not you.

## Urgency, at a glance

| Tier | Acknowledge | First action |
|---|---|---|
| **EMERGENCY** | Same day | Same day |
| **URGENT** | Within 24h | 2-3 days |
| **ROUTINE** | 2-3 days | 1-2 weeks |

Not sure which tier? Default to URGENT until intake tells you otherwise.

## Loop in an elder — only when

- Spiritual in nature (soul care, grief, doctrine, marriage/family struggle)
- Requires spiritual authority (discipline, restoration)
- Hits a policy threshold (e.g. benevolence that materially drains the fund; building spend over $12,500)

Mixed request? Deacons keep the tangible part. Only the spiritual part goes to a **named** elder, with a one-paragraph summary. Never tell a member to go find an elder themselves.

## The one hard rule

> **Every handoff: one named person → one named person, directly. A verbal yes, not an assumption.**

No group chats. No department emails. No "someone will pick this up."

---

## If you can't do this week

**Planned (you know in advance):** call your backup now, agree on coverage, tell the Chair, note it on the roster.

**Unplanned (mid-week, today):** backup activates immediately on their own — they don't wait to be asked. Backup tells the Chair. New requests route to the backup.

**Backup's unreachable too:** call the Chair directly. This should be rare — if it's happening, that's itself worth mentioning to the Chair afterward.
