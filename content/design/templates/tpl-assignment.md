# Assignment

**Who sends it:** Duty Deacon
**When:** After intake is complete and a specific helper or owner has been identified
**Channel:** Text or call to the owner; optionally a brief intro message to the member

---

## Part A — To the Owner / Helper

> Hey [Owner Name], I'm hoping you can take the lead on helping [Member Name]. Here's a quick summary:
>
> **Need:** [Brief description — what, how much, by when]
> **Urgency:** [e.g. Needs help by Friday / Not time-sensitive]
> **Background:** [Any relevant context — what they've already tried, sensitivity notes]
> **Best way to reach them:** [Phone / text / email — and best time]
>
> Can you reach out to [Member Name] by [target date/time]? Let me know if you have questions or if this doesn't work for you — I'll find someone else rather than leave it hanging. Thanks, [Deacon Name].

---

## Part B — To the Member (optional intro)

Only send if the member doesn't already know the owner, or if the handoff might otherwise feel abrupt.

> Hi [Name], I wanted to let you know that [Owner Name] will be your main point of contact from here. They'll be reaching out to you [by/around] [date/time]. Feel free to reach out to me if you don't hear from them or if anything else comes up.

---

## Notes
- Get a clear "yes" from the owner before telling the member someone is taking over. See [[tpl-acknowledgement]] for how to handle the gap.
- Include urgency level explicitly — don't assume the owner knows the SLA. See [[sla-and-urgency]].
- If no owner is available, escalate to the deacon lead rather than leaving it unassigned. See [[deacon-playbook]].
