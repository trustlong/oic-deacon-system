# Redirect to Elder

**Who sends it:** Duty Deacon (or the handling owner)
**When:** A spiritual need is present — grief, crisis of faith, marriage difficulty, need for pastoral counsel, request for prayer that goes beyond a quick intercession. See [[escalation-to-elders]] for full criteria.
**Note:** The deacon does not disengage. If tangible needs are also present, the deacon continues handling those. This is a warm hand-off, not a referral to the phone book.

---

## Part A — Summary to the Named Elder

Send or say this before connecting the elder to the member. Give the elder context so they can walk in prepared.

> Hey [Elder Name], I wanted to give you a heads-up about [Member Name]. They reached out to us about [tangible need — e.g. help with groceries], but in our conversation it became clear there's something deeper going on — [brief description: e.g. they're going through a really hard loss and mentioned feeling like they're losing their footing spiritually].
>
> I'm continuing to work on the [tangible side], so that piece is covered. But I think they could really use a pastoral presence from someone they trust. Would you be able to reach out to them? They're best reached by [phone/text/in-person], and [best time if known].
>
> I've already let them know you may be in touch. Thanks for caring for them with me.

---

## Part B — To the Member

Say or send this *after* you have confirmed the elder is willing and available — never before.

> [Name], I've been thinking about what you shared, and I spoke with [Elder Name] — one of our elders who I really trust. They'd love to connect with you. You should hear from them [today / this week / soon]. They know a little about what's going on so you won't have to start from scratch.
>
> In the meantime, I'm still working on [the tangible need] and will keep you posted. You're not alone in this.

---

## Notes
- Never tell a member to "go find an elder" or "reach out to leadership." The deacon makes the connection.
- Do not share more detail with the elder than the member would be comfortable with. When in doubt, ask the member first: "Would it be okay if I give [Elder Name] a little context?"
- If no elder is available right away, tell the member a specific name and a specific timeframe — not "someone will be in touch."
- See [[escalation-to-elders]] for the full protocol and elder availability.
