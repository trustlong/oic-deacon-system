# Not Eligible

**Who sends it:** Duty Deacon or Owner, after confirming with the deacon lead if uncertain
**When:** A request falls outside the scope of what the deacon team can provide — e.g. a benevolence exclusion, a type of need outside our capacity, or a request that requires professional services
**Channel:** Phone call strongly preferred for this message; text or email only if a call isn't possible

---

## Template — Out of Scope / Benevolence Exclusion

> [Name], thank you so much for trusting us with this. I want to be honest with you and not leave you waiting — what you've described falls outside what our deacon team is set up to help with directly. I'm sorry we can't do more on our end.
>
> I don't want to just leave you with a "no," though. [Choose one or more that applies:]
> - [Community resource or referral: e.g. "I know [Organization] helps with this — here's their contact: ____"]
> - ["I'd encourage you to also speak with [Elder/Pastor Name], who may know of other avenues."]
> - ["If your situation changes or there's a different way we can help, please reach back out — we're still here."]
>
> I'm sorry I don't have better news, and I hope you find the help you need. — [Deacon Name]

---

## Notes
- Never say "that's not our job." Say "that's outside what we're set up to do" and offer an alternative path.
- If you're unsure whether a request is out of scope, do not send this message yet — check with the deacon lead first. It's better to take a day to confirm than to wrongly turn someone away.
- For benevolence-specific exclusions, see [[benevolence/index|Benevolence Policy]].
- Document the decision in the request log with the reason, so we can identify patterns and gaps in our coverage over time.
- If a spiritual need is also present, do not send this template alone — combine with [[tpl-redirect-to-elder]].
