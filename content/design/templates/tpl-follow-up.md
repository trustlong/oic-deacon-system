# Follow-Up

**Who sends it:** Owner (the deacon or helper who took the request)
**When:** Shortly after the help was provided — typically 2–5 days, or within the window set in [[sla-and-urgency]]
**Channel:** Text or call (prefer the channel the member is most comfortable with)

---

## Template — Standard Check-In

> Hi [Name], this is [Owner Name]. I wanted to check in and make sure [the groceries arrived / the ride worked out / things got sorted — be specific] — did everything go okay?
>
> Is there anything else you're still needing, or anything I can do to help?

---

## Template — When You're Not Sure the Need Was Fully Met

> Hi [Name], I just wanted to follow up on [the help we arranged]. I want to make sure it actually landed the way it was supposed to — did you get what you needed?
>
> If anything fell short or there's still something open, please let me know. I'd rather know than have something slip through.

---

## Notes
- Be specific about *what* you're following up on — a generic "just checking in" can feel hollow.
- If the need wasn't met, re-engage immediately. Don't close the request until it's genuinely resolved.
- If the follow-up surfaces a new or ongoing need, create a new request rather than extending this one indefinitely.
- Once the member confirms the need is met, proceed to [[tpl-closure]].
- If you can't reach the member after two attempts, flag it in the request log and notify the duty deacon.
