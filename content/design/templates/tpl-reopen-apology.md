# Reopen / Apology

**Who sends it:** Duty Deacon or Deacon Lead (not the person who dropped it, unless they are best positioned to rebuild the relationship)
**When:** A request was stalled, forgotten, or fell through the cracks and is being re-engaged. See [[crack-detection]] for how these are identified.
**Channel:** Phone call first if at all possible. This message should not feel like a form letter.

---

## Template — Phone / Verbal

Use this as a guide, not a script. The most important things: be direct about what happened, don't over-explain, and focus on moving forward.

> "[Name], this is [Deacon Name] from [Church Name]. I'm calling because we dropped the ball — your request from [approximate date] about [need] didn't get the follow-through it deserved, and I'm sorry. That's on us, and I don't want to make excuses for it.
>
> I want to make sure we still help if you still need it. Is [the need] still open, or have you found another way to address it?
>
> [If yes:] Good — I'm going to personally make sure this gets handled. Can you walk me through where things stand now?
> [If no:] I'm glad you found help. I still want to apologize for leaving you hanging, and I hope you'll give us another chance to serve you well."

---

## Template — Text / Written (use only if a call isn't possible)

> Hi [Name], this is [Deacon Name] from [Church Name]. I'm reaching out because your request from around [date] about [need] didn't get the follow-up it deserved — we're sorry. We dropped the ball and we want to make it right.
>
> Is the need still open? If so, we'd like to help. Please reply here or call me at [number] — I'll make sure this gets proper attention. — [Deacon Name]

---

## Notes
- Do not send a written apology if a phone call is possible. Tone matters enormously here.
- Keep the apology simple and direct. One sentence of apology, then a question about the current situation — resist the urge to over-explain what went wrong.
- If the member is hurt or frustrated, listen before problem-solving. Acknowledge their experience before moving to solutions.
- After the call, update the request log immediately: note the conversation, confirm whether the need is still open, and re-assign with a new urgency target.
- Flag repeated patterns of dropped requests to the deacon lead — see [[crack-detection]].
