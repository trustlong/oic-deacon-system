# Communication Templates

These templates are starting points — not scripts. Adapt the language to fit the person and situation. The goal is for the member to feel *heard, not processed*. Keep messages short, warm, and specific to their name and need. Remove placeholders before sending.

**Tone guidance:** Humble, unhurried, personal. Avoid corporate-sounding phrases ("your request has been received," "we will follow up accordingly"). Write like a caring neighbor, not a help desk.

**Format guidance:** Most messages go by text. Keep texts to 3–5 sentences. Email can be slightly longer but still concise. In-person adaptations should feel like natural conversation — use the template as a mental outline, not a script to read aloud.
