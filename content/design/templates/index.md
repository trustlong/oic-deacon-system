> **Goal:** ready-to-use message templates so responders communicate consistently and warmly at every step. Fill these in as the workflow ([[routing-and-functions]], [[sla-and-urgency]]) is finalized.

## Templates to write

- [[tpl-acknowledgement]] — "We received your request and I'm your point of contact."
- [[tpl-assignment]] — to the member/helper being asked to help.
- [[tpl-follow-up]] — checking the need was met (ties to the Benevolence follow-up step).
- [[tpl-redirect-to-elder]] — gently handing a spiritual need to an elder ([[escalation-to-elders]]).
- [[tpl-closure]] — confirming resolution and closing the loop.
- [[tpl-not-eligible]] — a kind explanation when a request is out of scope (e.g. benevolence [[benevolence-whats-not-covered|exclusions]]).

Each template should be short, personal, and fill-in-the-blank. Keep tone consistent with our church's voice.
