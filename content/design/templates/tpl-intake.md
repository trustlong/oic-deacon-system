# Intake

**Who sends it:** Duty Deacon
**When:** After the acknowledgement, when you need more information before handing off to an owner
**Channel:** Text, call, or in-person — whichever the member prefers

---

## Template — Text / Written

> Hi [Name], thank you for letting us know. I want to make sure we help well, so I have a few quick questions:
>
> 1. Can you tell me a little more about what's needed — specifically [what / how much / how often]?
> 2. Is there a deadline or a date this needs to happen by?
> 3. Have you already tried anything or been in touch with anyone else about this?
> 4. What's the best way and time to reach you?
>
> No rush — just reply whenever you're able.

---

## Template — Verbal / In-Person (outline)

Use these as natural conversation prompts, not a list to read aloud:

- "Tell me a bit more about what's going on — what does the need look like right now?"
- "Is there a timeframe that matters — like, is something happening soon?"
- "Have you already reached out to anyone else, or tried anything so far?"
- "What's the best way to stay in touch with you — text, call, email?"

---

## Notes
- Keep the tone conversational. You're a neighbor asking how to help, not completing a form.
- Only ask what you need. If the request is already clear, skip to [[tpl-assignment]].
- Record answers in the request log before handing off. The owner should not have to re-ask the same questions.
- If anything in the conversation reveals a spiritual need, see [[tpl-redirect-to-elder]].
