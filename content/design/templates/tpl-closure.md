# Closure

**Who sends it:** Owner
**When:** After the member confirms (or it is clear) that the need has been fully met
**Channel:** Text, or in-person if that's where the relationship lives

---

## Template — Standard Closure

> Hi [Name], I'm so glad we were able to help with [need]. It's been a privilege to walk with you through this.
>
> We're closing this out on our end, but please don't hesitate to reach out if you ever need anything — that's what we're here for. You can always contact me, or reach our deacon team through [contact method].
>
> Take care, [Owner Name].

---

## Template — Brief / Lighter Touch (for simpler requests)

> Hi [Name], glad we could help! Closing this out on our end — please reach out anytime if something else comes up.

---

## Notes
- The closure message is not a formality — it's a moment to affirm the person, not just the transaction.
- Tailor the warmth to the weight of the situation. A meal delivery gets a lighter close than a months-long crisis.
- Before sending, confirm in the request log that:
  - The need was actually met (not just that help was sent)
  - Any follow-up promises made along the way were kept
  - The status is being updated to **Closed**
- If the member expresses ongoing needs at closure, open a new request rather than re-opening this one.
