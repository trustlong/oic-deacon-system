# Acknowledgement

**Who sends it:** Duty Deacon
**When:** As soon as a request comes in — ideally within the urgency target window (see [[sla-and-urgency]])
**Channel:** Text (preferred), or match how the request arrived

---

## Template — Standard (non-urgent)

> Hi [Name], this is [Deacon Name] from [Church Name]. I just heard from [person/source] that you might need some help with [need]. I'm glad you reached out. I'll be your point of contact while we get this sorted out, and I'll be back in touch by [date/time]. If anything changes before then, feel free to text or call me directly at this number.

---

## Template — Urgent

> Hi [Name], this is [Deacon Name] from [Church Name]. I just heard you're dealing with [need] right now — I want to make sure we get you help quickly. I'm reaching out to the right people as we speak and will follow up with you within [timeframe, e.g. a couple of hours]. Please don't hesitate to reach back out to me directly if you need anything in the meantime.

---

## Notes
- Do not promise a specific outcome yet — only that you are engaged and they have a named contact.
- If you'll be handing off to an owner, you can say "I may connect you with [Name], who will be handling this directly" — but only once that handoff is confirmed. See [[tpl-assignment]].
- For spiritual needs surfacing alongside a tangible need, do not defer — see [[tpl-redirect-to-elder]].
