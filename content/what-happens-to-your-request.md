When you reach out to the deacons, here is what happens — from your point of view.

---

## 1. A deacon personally receives your request

As soon as your request comes in, a deacon takes ownership of it. This is not an automated system — a real person reads what you've shared and will reach out to you.

**Urgent needs** (something at risk today or this week) get same-day attention.
**All other requests** receive a prompt acknowledgement — typically within a day or two — so you know we have it and someone is on it.

*Not sure what counts as urgent? When in doubt, tell us, and we will sort it out with you.*

---

## 2. They learn what you need

The deacon who receives your request will contact you to understand the situation fully — what you need, any constraints, and how best to help. This might be a quick conversation on a Sunday, a short phone call, or a few messages back and forth. We want to understand before we act so that the help we find actually fits.

---

## 3. They connect you with the right person to help

Once the deacon understands your need, they find the right helper — whether that is another deacon, a church member with the right skill or resource, a community program, or a combination. They make that connection for you. You do not have to track anyone down yourself.

---

## 4. That person stays with it until the need is met

Whoever steps in to help does not disappear after the first contact. They follow through until the need is actually resolved, and they check back to make sure things worked out.

---

## 5. If it is a spiritual need, you are connected to an elder

If what you are carrying turns out to be — or includes — a spiritual need (counseling, prayer, a question of faith, a marriage or family struggle), the deacon will warmly connect you to one of our elders. Elders carry that kind of pastoral care. The deacon does not abandon you; they simply make sure the right person is involved. See [[handbook-tangible-vs-spiritual|how we direct different kinds of needs]].

---

## 6. Nothing gets forgotten

Every request is tracked by the deacons so that nothing slips through. If a need is taking longer than expected or circumstances change, you can always reach back out to any deacon and someone will get you an update.
