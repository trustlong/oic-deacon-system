# **OIC Church Building & Renovation Fund Policy**

*(Adopted 08/03/2025)*

1. ### **Purpose**

The Building & Renovation Fund (the “Fund”) exists to support the long-term stewardship of OIC Church’s physical property, including maintenance, renovation, and facility upgrades, in order to advance the mission of OIC Church in a sustainable, transparent, and God-glorifying way.

2. ### **Oversight and Administration**

In accordance with the OIC Church Bylaws and the Deacons Handbook, the Fund shall be overseen and administered as follows:

1. **Deacon Board Authority**: Oversight of the Fund shall be vested in the Deacon Board, who shall act under the accountability of the Elder Board and in coordination with the Finance Department (*Handbook*, pp. 18–20; *Bylaws*, Art. VI.2, Art. IV.6).

2. **Fund Subcommittee**: The Deacon Board may appoint a subcommittee (e.g., “Building Fund Committee”) to advise and execute responsibilities, including planning, implementation, and reporting.

3. **Elders Oversight**: The Elders (or their designee) shall provide operational oversight to ensure the Fund’s use aligns with OIC’s mission and ministry needs (*Bylaws*, Art. IV.2, IV.6).

4. **Donor Conditions**: Any donor-designated contributions must be consistent with this policy and subject to board discretion in execution. Donor requests may be honored if feasible but are non-binding.

5. **Fund Closure**: Should the Fund be closed or restructured, the Deacon Board shall recommend a plan for disposition of the balance, subject to approval by the Elder Board, ensuring that any remaining balances are allocated according to the purposes most aligned with OIC’s mission and ministry.

3. ### **Scope of Use**

The Fund is designated exclusively for:

1. Deferred maintenance and major component repair/replacement (e.g., roofing, HVAC, flooring, plumbing)

2. Facility improvements to support growth, accessibility, or ministry expansion

3. Emergency capital needs when urgent and unplanned repairs are required

Expenditures for routine maintenance or operational supplies shall be budgeted separately in the **Operating Fund**.

4. ### **Reserve Study and Long-Term Planning**

A professional Reserve Study should be conducted every five years, with in-house updates annually. The study shall include:

1. A full inventory of building components (e.g., HVAC, roof, parking lot)

2. Assessment of remaining useful life and current replacement cost

3. Recommendations for annual reserve contributions

4. A target Fully Funded Balance and Percent Funded benchmark

While a professional Reserve Study is preferred, an in-house reserve study is an acceptable and appropriate alternative when budget constraints make a professional study impractical.

The Deacon Board will review Reserve Study results annually and incorporate the recommendations into the budgeting process.

5. ### **Budgeting and Funding Contributions**

Each year, during the budget preparation process, the Finance Department and Deacon Board shall:

1. Review the Reserve Study's recommended annual contribution

2. Identify capital projects planned for the upcoming year

3. Determine the proposed annual Fund contribution and submit for Elder Board review and congregational awareness (*Bylaws*, Art. VI.3, Art. VIII.1; *Handbook*, pp. 15, 18–20)

4. Maintain a Threshold Funding Level of at least **80% funded**, with catch-up plans if shortfalls occur over a 5-year window

6. ### **Project Approval Process**

Assuming a $250,000 annual budget, all expenditures must be approved according to the following tiers:

| Amount | Approval Required |
| ----- | ----- |
| **\<$1,250** | Operations Chair and Treasurer |
| **$1,250–$12,500** | Deacon Board approval |
| **\>$12,500** | Congregational approval, upon recommendation of Deacons and concurrence of Elders |

Additional requirements:

1. All checks must be signed by **two authorized individuals**, excluding the checkbook keeper.

7. ### **Reporting and Transparency**

The Building Fund Subcommittee or Finance Department shall:

1. Prepare an **annual Building Fund Report**, including beginning balance, contributions, interest, expenses, ending balance, and reserve study status

2. Publish project updates and fund status during at least one **congregational meeting per year** (*Bylaws*, Art. VIII.1; *Handbook*, p. 15)

8. ### **Fundraising and Communication**

The Building Fund may be promoted in designated giving campaigns, where specific projects and costs are presented transparently, linking needs to Reserve Study goals.

Donors will be clearly informed that contributions are to a **restricted fund under church leadership oversight** and are **not refundable**.
