**ONE IN CHRIST — CHURCH CONSTITUTION**

## Introduction

We declare and affirm these principles in the light of the Holy Scripture to govern this body in an orderly manner. These principles are made to preserve and secure the principles of our faith; preserve the liberties of each individual church member; and ensure freedom of action of independence from any religious body or organization.

## Article I — Name

The name of this church shall be One In Christ (OIC), with facilities located at 1595 Turkey Foot Road.

## Article II — Purpose Statement

We are a body of Christians from diverse ethnic and cultural backgrounds united for the purpose of worshipping God through Christ, edifying the saints, and evangelizing the nations.

## Article III — Mission Statement

The missions of One In Christ (OIC) are: We desire to live under the authority of the Word of God, in order to worship the triune God and love each other as a whole body of Christ, so that we spread the Gospel to the world.

- **Word:** 2 Timothy 4:2 — to preach the word, correct, rebuke, and encourage.
- **Worship:** Matthew 4:10 — to worship God, make Him the first priority in our life, who is worthy of all praise and glory. We will celebrate God's activity in our lives.
- **Wholeness:** Ephesians 2:19 — to fellowship together. We will bring people to God's family.
- **World:** Matthew 28:19-20 — Go into all the world and make disciples.

While these four missions are distinct by nature, they are of equal importance.

## Article IV — Faith Statement

We are a partner of the Southern Baptist Conservatives of Virginia, which is a fellowship of Southern Baptist Convention churches in the Commonwealth of Virginia. We endorse the 2000 Baptist Faith and Message (http://www.sbc.net/bfm2000/bfm2000.asp).

## Article V — Government

Under the Lordship of Christ, the government of One In Christ (OIC) is vested in the members who compose her (see [[bylaws-membership|Bylaws, Article I — Membership]]). The church is subject to the control of no other ecclesiastical body, but recognizes and sustains the obligations of mutual counsel and cooperation, which are common among Southern Baptist Churches. While maintaining local autonomy, One In Christ (OIC) will cooperate with and support the Southern Baptist Convention and other groups as desired.

As a local church, the style of government will be elder-led and congregational, submissive to the Word and Will of God as best understood and congregationally affirmed. All members have the responsibility of participating in the decision-making process, including sharing their thoughts, ideas, and concerns. The offices of the church will be Elder and Deacon (see [[bylaws-elders|Bylaws, Article II — The Office of Elder]] and [[bylaws-deacons|Bylaws, Article II — The Office of Deacon]]).

## Article VI — Non-profit Status and Liquidation

One In Christ (OIC) is a not-for-profit corporation under the laws of the Commonwealth of Virginia. In the event of dissolution of the church, all of its assets and property of every nature whatsoever shall be paid over and transferred at the direction of the elders to the Southern Baptist Conservatives of Virginia.

## Article VII — Amendment

This Constitution may be amended provided the proposed amendment shall have been presented in writing at a special called or a regular church member's meeting and then circulated to the entire church membership. The vote shall be taken not less than 30 days after formal presentation of the constitution to the church. Amendments to this Constitution shall require **100% approval** of all members of the church who are qualified to vote and are present at the meeting with a quorum to discuss and vote for this matter.

## Article VIII — Adoption

This Constitution shall be considered adopted and will take immediate effect when 100% of the members present vote and affirm this constitution at the church member's meeting. This vote shall be taken not less than 30 days after formal presentation of the constitution to the church. This constitution abolishes, supersedes, and takes the place of any constitution, rules, and bylaws that preceded it. A copy of this constitution shall be made available to church members on request.
