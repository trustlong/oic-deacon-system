# **OIC Transportation Service: Policy & Guidelines**

## **Purpose & Scope**

The **OIC Transportation Service** helps individuals attend **church-sponsored worship services and activities**. It is a volunteer ministry of One In Christ Church, not a commercial transportation service.

Priority is given to college students, teenagers (with parent or guardian consent), seniors, and those with genuine transportation needs.

## **Scheduling & Return**

Riders must be **ready at the designated pickup time and location**. Vehicles may depart if a rider is late. Please give advance notice for any change or cancellation.

**Return rides are not guaranteed** — OIC will assist when possible, but riders should plan for their own way home.

## **Safety & Conduct**

For everyone's safety:

- **Seat belts must be worn** while the vehicle is in motion.
- Remain seated, store belongings securely, and avoid distracting the driver.
- Alcohol, tobacco, or illegal substances are **not permitted**.
- Riders are expected to show **respect and cooperation** toward drivers and passengers.
- Disruptive or unsafe behavior may result in suspension of ride privileges.
- Minors must have a signed waiver on file from a parent or guardian.

## **Drivers & Vehicles**

- All drivers are approved **volunteers** of the OIC church.
- Vehicles must be insured, registered, and properly maintained.
- The service complies with all applicable state and local traffic laws.

## **Rights & Limitations**

OIC Transportation Service may **deny, limit, or cancel** rides based on safety, capacity, weather, or other valid reasons. Policies may be updated as needed.

This ministry is provided as a **voluntary service of One In Christ Church**, not as a public or for-hire transportation entity.
