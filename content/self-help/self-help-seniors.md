This page gathers practical resources for seniors in our church family — covering transportation, health benefits, in-home support, meals, and more. The deacons keep it current.

**Need personal help?** [[need-help|Ask a deacon.]] We are glad to sit with you and work through any of this together, or to find someone who can.

## Transportation

Getting to appointments, church, and errands matters.

- **Uber / Lyft** — ride services available via smartphone app; if you need help setting up the app, ask a deacon or church member
- **GoGo Grandparent** — a service that lets you call Uber/Lyft by phone (no smartphone needed): [gogograndparent.com](https://gogograndparent.com) or call **1-855-464-6872**
- **Greater Lynchburg Transit Company (GLTC)** — local bus service: [gltconline.com](https://www.gltconline.com)
- **JAUNT / Valley Metro** — regional transit that may include paratransit or senior-specific options

**GLTC Paratransit** — for those whose disability prevents using regular bus routes; covers all of Lynchburg plus a 3/4-mile radius beyond routes. $4 per one-way trip. Book by 5pm the evening before; rides run Mon–Fri 8:30am–5pm. Call (434) 455-5099.

**Church ride coordination:** Bob You coordinates rides for members who need one. [[need-help|Ask a deacon]] to connect you with him.

If you need a ride and the options above don't work for your situation, [[need-help|ask a deacon]] — church members regularly offer rides.

## Medicare Help

Medicare is complicated. Free counseling is available.

- **Medicare.gov** — the official source for plan comparisons, enrollment, and coverage questions: [medicare.gov](https://www.medicare.gov)
- **1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227)** — 24/7 helpline

**Free local Medicare counseling (no cost, no sales pitch):**

- **Virginia SHIP (State Health Insurance Assistance Program)** — trained volunteer counselors help you understand your Medicare options for free: [seniorsblueprint.virginia.gov/insurance](https://www.seniorsblueprint.virginia.gov/insurance/) or call **1-800-552-3402**
- **VICAP (Virginia Insurance Counseling and Assistance Program)** — part of Virginia SHIP, specifically focused on Medicare and supplemental insurance

**Local VICAP provider:** Central Virginia Alliance for Community Living (CVACL), 501 12th St, Suite A, Lynchburg, VA 24504. Phone (434) 385-9070. Counseling is available by phone or in-person appointment. If you have trouble reaching them, the statewide VICAP intake line is (804) 343-3014.

## In-Home Support

If you need help with daily activities at home:

- **Eldercare Locator** — a free government service to find local in-home care, adult day programs, and caregiver support: [eldercare.acl.gov](https://eldercare.acl.gov) or call **1-800-677-1116**
- **Virginia Department for Aging and Rehabilitative Services (DARS)** — connects seniors with in-home services: [vda.virginia.gov](https://www.vda.virginia.gov)
- **Area Agencies on Aging** — local organizations that coordinate services for seniors; find yours through the Eldercare Locator above

**Local Area Agency on Aging:** Central Virginia Alliance for Community Living (CVACL) — same office as the VICAP counselors above: 501 12th St, Suite A, Lynchburg, VA 24504, (434) 385-9070. They coordinate services, in-home support referrals, respite care, and caregiver support for the Lynchburg region.

## Meal Programs

- **Meals on Wheels America** — home-delivered meals for seniors who have difficulty cooking; find a local program: [mealsonwheelsamerica.org](https://www.mealsonwheelsamerica.org)
- **Senior dining programs** — many communities offer congregate meal programs (eating with others at a senior center); the Eldercare Locator can help you find one
- **SNAP (food stamps)** — seniors often qualify; applying is worth it: [fns.usda.gov/snap](https://www.fns.usda.gov/snap)
- **Feeding America food bank locator:** [feedingamerica.org/find-your-local-foodbank](https://www.feedingamerica.org/find-your-local-foodbank)

**Meals on Wheels of Greater Lynchburg** — 605 Clay St, Lynchburg, VA (mailing: PO Box 1388, Lynchburg, VA 24505). Phone (434) 847-0796. Serves Lynchburg, Amherst, Bedford, and Campbell counties: [mealslynchburg.org](https://mealslynchburg.org)

## Technology Help

Technology can feel overwhelming, but it also connects you to people and services.

- **Older Adults Technology Services (OATS) / AARP** — free tech training resources for older adults: [oats.org](https://oats.org)
- **Lifeline Program** — if your income is low, you may qualify for a free or discounted phone: [lifelinesupport.org](https://www.lifelinesupport.org)
- **Church members** — many of our younger members are glad to help set up a phone, tablet, or video call. [[need-help|Ask a deacon]] and we will connect you with someone patient and willing.

## Financial Assistance

- **211.org** — dial or text **211** to find local help with utilities, rent, food, and other needs: [211.org](https://www.211.org)
- **LIHEAP (Low Income Home Energy Assistance)** — help with heating and cooling bills: [acf.hhs.gov/ocs/low-income-home-energy-assistance-program-liheap](https://www.acf.hhs.gov/ocs/low-income-home-energy-assistance-program-liheap)
- **Benefits.gov** — find federal benefits you may be entitled to: [benefits.gov](https://www.benefits.gov)

For church-based financial help as a last resort, see [[benevolence/index|Church Benevolence]], [[benevolence-who-qualifies|who qualifies]], and [[benevolence-eligible-needs|what it covers]].
