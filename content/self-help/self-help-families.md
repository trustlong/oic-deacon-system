**Need personal help?** [[need-help|Ask a deacon.]] Whether it's coordinating meals after a baby or figuring out how to pay the rent, we want to help you carry it.

## Meal Coordination

When a baby is born, someone comes home from the hospital, or there is a loss — a meal is one of the most tangible things a community can offer.

- **Meal Train** — free online tool for organizing meal deliveries from friends and church members: [mealtrain.com](https://www.mealtrain.com)
- **SignUpGenius** — another free tool for coordinating volunteer sign-ups (meals, childcare, rides, etc.): [signupgenius.com](https://www.signupgenius.com)

If you need meals coordinated and are not sure how to set it up, [[need-help|ask a deacon]] — we can help organize it through the church.

## Childcare & Parenting Resources

- **Child Care Aware of America** — find licensed childcare in your area and understand your options: [childcareaware.org](https://www.childcareaware.org)
- **Virginia Department of Social Services — Child Care:** information on state-subsidized childcare assistance: [dss.virginia.gov/family/cc](https://www.dss.virginia.gov/family/cc/)
- **Head Start** — federally funded early childhood program for income-qualifying families with children under 5: [eclkc.ohs.acf.hhs.gov](https://eclkc.ohs.acf.hhs.gov)
- **WIC (Women, Infants, and Children)** — nutrition support for pregnant and postpartum women and children under 5: [fns.usda.gov/wic](https://www.fns.usda.gov/wic)

## Utility & Rent Assistance

When the bills pile up, these are places to turn before they become a crisis:

- **211.org** — dial or text **211** to find local help with rent, utilities, and other emergency needs: [211.org](https://www.211.org)
- **LIHEAP (Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program)** — federal program that helps with heating and cooling costs: [acf.hhs.gov/ocs/low-income-home-energy-assistance-program-liheap](https://www.acf.hhs.gov/ocs/low-income-home-energy-assistance-program-liheap)
- **Virginia Department of Housing and Community Development** — rental assistance programs: [dhcd.virginia.gov](https://www.dhcd.virginia.gov)

## Food Assistance

- **SNAP (food stamps)** — apply at your state's DSS office; check eligibility and how to apply: [fns.usda.gov/snap](https://www.fns.usda.gov/snap)
- **Feeding America food bank locator:** [feedingamerica.org/find-your-local-foodbank](https://www.feedingamerica.org/find-your-local-foodbank)
- **211.org** — lists local food pantries and emergency food near you: [211.org](https://www.211.org)
