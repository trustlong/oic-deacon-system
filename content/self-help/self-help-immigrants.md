Welcome. If you are new to the United States or recently arrived in the Lynchburg area, this page points to free and reliable resources to help you get settled. The deacons keep this list current — if something is out of date or you know of a resource we should add, please tell us.

**Need personal help navigating any of this?** [[need-help|Ask a deacon.]] You do not have to figure it out alone.

## English (ESL) Classes

Learning or improving your English opens many doors.

- **USA Learns** — free online ESL courses at any level: [usalearns.org](https://www.usalearns.org)
- **Duolingo** — free language app for building vocabulary and basic conversation: [duolingo.com](https://www.duolingo.com)
- **ESL in America (government resource)** — find adult ESL programs near you: [literacydirectory.org](https://www.literacydirectory.org)

## Driver's License & State ID (Virginia)

- **Virginia DMV** — official information on getting a Virginia driver's license or ID card, including what documents you need: [dmv.virginia.gov](https://www.dmv.virginia.gov)
  - Look for "New Residents" and "Driver's License & ID" sections
  - Virginia requires a REAL ID-compliant document for federal purposes; the DMV website explains requirements for non-citizens

## Social Security Card & Number

A Social Security Number (SSN) or Individual Taxpayer Identification Number (ITIN) is needed for work and many other purposes.

- **Social Security Administration** — how to apply for a card, what documents are needed, and what immigrants are eligible: [ssa.gov/ssnumber](https://www.ssa.gov/ssnumber/)
- For ITIN (if you are not eligible for an SSN): [IRS ITIN information](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/individual-taxpayer-identification-number)

> **Local resources (deacons: add the nearest Social Security Administration office address and hours here)**

## Immigration Paperwork & Status

- **USCIS (U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services)** — official government source for forms, status checks, and information about green cards, naturalization, work authorization, and more: [uscis.gov](https://www.uscis.gov)
- **USCIS Case Status Online** — check the status of a pending application: [egov.uscis.gov/casestatus](https://egov.uscis.gov/casestatus/landing.do)

> **Important:** This page provides referral information only. Immigration law is complex and errors can have serious consequences. For legal advice about your specific situation, seek a qualified immigration attorney or accredited representative. The deacons can help you find a trustworthy referral.

## Setting Up a Bank Account

Having a bank account makes receiving pay, paying bills, and managing money much easier.

- Many banks and credit unions allow immigrants to open accounts with a passport and ITIN — ask at the bank directly
- **MyMoney.gov** — free government financial education resources: [mymoney.gov](https://www.mymoney.gov)

## Phone Service

- **Lifeline Program** — if your income is low, you may qualify for discounted phone or internet service: [lifelinesupport.org](https://www.lifelinesupport.org)
- Prepaid ("pay-as-you-go") SIM cards from major carriers (T-Mobile, AT&T, Tracfone) are available without a Social Security Number and are a good starting option

---

## Utilities

- **211.org** — call or text **211** to find help with utility bills, deposits, and emergency energy assistance in your area: [211.org](https://www.211.org)
- **LIHEAP (Low Income Home Energy Assistance)** — federal program that helps with heating and cooling costs: [acf.hhs.gov/ocs/low-income-home-energy-assistance-program-liheap](https://www.acf.hhs.gov/ocs/low-income-home-energy-assistance-program-liheap)

## Finding Groceries & Food Assistance

- **Feeding America food bank locator** — find a food bank or food pantry near you: [feedingamerica.org/find-your-local-foodbank](https://www.feedingamerica.org/find-your-local-foodbank)
- **SNAP (food stamps)** — if you are eligible based on your immigration status and income, SNAP helps buy groceries: [fns.usda.gov/snap](https://www.fns.usda.gov/snap)
- **WIC** — for pregnant women, new mothers, and children under 5: [fns.usda.gov/wic](https://www.fns.usda.gov/wic)
- **211.org** — also lists local food pantries: [211.org](https://www.211.org)
