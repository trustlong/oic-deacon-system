If money has become a crisis — a past-due bill, an empty pantry, a shutoff notice — there is help available. This page walks through what to try and in what order.

**Please do not wait until things are desperate.** The earlier you reach out, the more options there are. If you are not sure where to start, [[need-help|ask a deacon]] and we will help you figure it out.

---

## Start Here: 211

**Dial or text 211** — or visit [211.org](https://www.211.org) — to speak with a local social services navigator. They can connect you to:

- Utility assistance (electric, gas, water)
- Emergency rent help
- Food pantries and meal programs
- Medical and dental resources
- And much more

211 is free, confidential, and available in multiple languages. It is often the fastest way to find what is available in your specific area.

## Utility Bills

If your utilities are at risk of shutoff — or already off:

- **LIHEAP (Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program)** — federal program for heating and cooling bill help: [acf.hhs.gov/ocs/low-income-home-energy-assistance-program-liheap](https://www.acf.hhs.gov/ocs/low-income-home-energy-assistance-program-liheap)
- **Virginia LIHEAP** — apply through your local Department of Social Services: [dss.virginia.gov/benefit/liheap.cgi](https://www.dss.virginia.gov/benefit/liheap.cgi)
- **211.org** — lists additional local utility assistance programs

**Lynchburg Department of Social Services** — 99 9th St, Lynchburg, VA 24505. Phone (434) 455-5850. Handles LIHEAP/fuel assistance intake. For SNAP/TANF/Medicaid, the statewide line is 855-635-4370.

## Rent Assistance

- **211.org** — the first call for emergency rent help; navigators know what is currently funded locally
- **Virginia Department of Housing and Community Development** — rental assistance programs: [dhcd.virginia.gov](https://www.dhcd.virginia.gov)
- **HUD-approved housing counselors** — free help navigating rental crises: [hud.gov/findacounselor](https://www.hud.gov/findacounselor)

## Food Assistance

- **SNAP (food stamps)** — monthly benefits to buy groceries if you qualify; apply through Virginia DSS: [commonhelp.virginia.gov](https://www.commonhelp.virginia.gov)
- **WIC** — nutrition support for pregnant and postpartum women and children under 5: [fns.usda.gov/wic](https://www.fns.usda.gov/wic)
- **Feeding America food bank locator** — find a food bank or pantry near you: [feedingamerica.org/find-your-local-foodbank](https://www.feedingamerica.org/find-your-local-foodbank)
- **211.org** — also lists local food pantries with current hours

**Lynchburg-area food pantries** *(call ahead — hours and any ID/income requirements vary and change)*:

## Medical Costs

- **HealthCare.gov** — find marketplace insurance plans; you may qualify for subsidies: [healthcare.gov](https://www.healthcare.gov)
- **Medicaid** — free or low-cost health coverage for qualifying low-income individuals and families; apply through Virginia: [coverva.org](https://www.coverva.org)
- **HRSA Health Centers** — federally funded community health clinics that serve patients regardless of ability to pay; find one near you: [findahealthcenter.hrsa.gov](https://findahealthcenter.hrsa.gov)
- **NeedyMeds** — help finding patient assistance programs for prescription drugs: [needymeds.org](https://www.needymeds.org)

## Budgeting & Financial Counseling

Getting a handle on income and expenses can prevent a crisis from deepening.

- **CFPB (Consumer Financial Protection Bureau)** — free budgeting tools and financial guides: [consumerfinance.gov](https://www.consumerfinance.gov)
- **MyMoney.gov** — government financial literacy resource: [mymoney.gov](https://www.mymoney.gov)
- **NFCC (National Foundation for Credit Counseling)** — find a nonprofit credit counselor for free or low-cost help: [nfcc.org](https://www.nfcc.org) or call **1-800-388-2227**
- **Benefits.gov** — find federal assistance programs you may qualify for: [benefits.gov](https://www.benefits.gov)
