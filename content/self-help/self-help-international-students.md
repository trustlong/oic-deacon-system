## Arriving for the First Time

### Airport & Getting to Lynchburg

- **Lynchburg Regional Airport (LYH)** is the local airport, but flights often connect through Charlotte (CLT), Washington Dulles (IAD), or Richmond (RIC)
- Let a deacon or church contact know your arrival flight and time — we can often arrange a pickup or point you to transportation options
- **Amtrak** serves Lynchburg if you are arriving from a major city: [amtrak.com](https://www.amtrak.com)

### Your School's International Student Office

For anything related to your visa status, I-20, CPT/OPT authorization, travel signatures, or other immigration paperwork, **your school's international student office is your first and most important contact.** They are the official resource and the only people authorized to advise on your visa status.

Common offices:
- Liberty University: Center for Global Engagement
- Lynchburg University: Center for Global Education
- Randolph College: Office of International Programs

*(Check your school's website for current contact information.)*

## Transportation

Getting around without a car takes planning, but it is doable.

- **Greater Lynchburg Transit Company (GLTC)** — local bus service: check routes and schedules at [gltconline.com](https://www.gltconline.com)
- **Uber / Lyft** — available in the Lynchburg area for on-demand rides
- **Enterprise, Hertz, and other rental agencies** are available locally if you have a valid license
- **Carpooling** — church members often coordinate rides; speak to a deacon about connecting you with someone who passes near your school

## Finding Furniture & Household Goods

Setting up a place to live can be expensive. These options can help:

- **FreeCycle** — free items given away locally: [freecycle.org](https://www.freecycle.org)
- **Facebook Marketplace** and local "buy nothing" groups often have furniture, kitchenware, and bedding
- **Thrift stores** (Goodwill, Salvation Army, and others) offer low-cost household goods

## Summer Storage

If you need to store belongings over the summer instead of shipping them home:

- Some apartment complexes offer storage options — ask your landlord
- Several storage unit companies operate in the Lynchburg area (Public Storage, CubeSmart, and local options)
- Ask a deacon — sometimes church members can help with short-term storage

## Connecting with the Church Community

- **Sunday services** — the best way to meet people; introduce yourself and someone will introduce you to others
- **Cell groups** — small groups that meet weekly in homes; ask a deacon about groups near your campus
- **Church events** — we host gatherings specifically to connect newcomers; watch the bulletin or ask a deacon what is coming up

You are not just a visitor here. We want you to feel at home.
