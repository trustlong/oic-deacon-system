Welcome. This is the home of the **OIC Deacon Response System** — how our deacons serve the tangible needs of our church family, following the pattern of **Acts 6**.

> *"Elders lead ministry. Deacons facilitate ministry. The congregation does ministry."*

## I have a need

If you (or someone you know in the church) have a practical need — financial, logistical, a repair, a ride, a meal, help with something — start here:

➡️ **[Fill out our request form](https://script.google.com/macros/s/AKfycbymhYETeXtj8m8Lnfi3GDx4Zj90PC4kU3gLq0R2JstgTjIke9p0H1Ogd0e2VJlmyRCq5Q/exec)** — or read **[[need-help|Need Help? Start here]]** first.

For spiritual care, counseling, or prayer, those are entrusted to our **elders** — see [[handbook-tangible-vs-spiritual|how needs are directed]].

## How our deacons are organized

Our deacons serve through **four core functions**. Each request is routed to the function (or functions) best able to help:

- **[[handbook-administration|Administration]]** — records, meetings, correspondence, HR
- **[[handbook-operations|Operations]]** — facilities, repairs, security, setup, transportation
- **[[handbook-communications|Communications]]** — website, media, connecting people to help
- **[[handbook-finance|Finance]]** — budget, giving, benevolence, building fund
