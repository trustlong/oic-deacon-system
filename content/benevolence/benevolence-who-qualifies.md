# Who Qualifies

Benevolence assistance is available to **active covenant members in good standing** only. The following gates all apply.

## Eligibility Gates

1. **Active covenant member** — The applicant must be a professing, baptized member of OIC Church who faithfully attends public worship and ministries and supports the church financially. (Bylaws, Article 1, Section 1.) See [[bylaws-membership|Membership & Good Standing]].

2. **Not under church discipline** — Members who are dismissed or currently under church discipline are not eligible. (Bylaws, Article 1, Sections 3–4.)

3. **Fund is a last resort** — The applicant must first have exhausted reasonable personal resources: personal savings, support from family (per 1 Tim. 5:4, 8), applicable insurance, and government or other assistance programs. The fund fills the remaining gap, not the first stop.

4. **Good-faith effort to improve** — The applicant must be actively working to resolve the underlying difficulty — for example, seeking employment, budgeting, or pursuing counseling — rather than relying continuously on church aid.

5. **Willing to work (if able-bodied)** — Someone who is able to work but unwilling to do so does not normally qualify, in keeping with 2 Thessalonians 3:10. Underlying issues (addiction, persistent irresponsibility) may require counseling as a condition of assistance.

## A Note on Humility and Dignity

The application process is designed to be as barrier-free as possible. Asking for help is treated with understanding, not judgment. A team member can assist anyone who needs help completing the form.
