# Benevolence Fund

OIC Church's Benevolence Fund provides short-term, crisis assistance to active covenant members facing a genuine financial emergency. It is a **last resort** — intended to bridge an urgent gap after personal savings, family support, and insurance have been exhausted. All aid is given as a gift (not a loan) and is disbursed with compassion, accountability, and elder oversight.
