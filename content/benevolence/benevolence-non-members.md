# Help for Non-Members

Non-members and the broader community are **not eligible for disbursements** from the Benevolence Fund. The fund is reserved for active covenant members of OIC Church, following the biblical pattern of the church caring first for "the saints" (Galatians 6:10; Romans 12:13).

## Limited Mercy Aid

OIC Church still desires to show Christ's love to all who come to the door. When a stranger or non-member requests help, the church may offer **limited mercy aid outside the fund**, including:

- A grocery gift card
- A bag of non-perishable food (from the campus pantry or "blessing box")
- A referral to a trusted local ministry, food pantry, shelter, or counseling service

The church maintains an updated list of local charities and ministries to which non-members can be referred for more substantial or ongoing help.

## No Cash to Walk-Ins

The church does **not** give cash to walk-in requests. On-hand items — grocery vouchers, transit passes, hygiene kits — may be offered for immediate relief.

## Why This Distinction

This policy allows responsible stewardship of the congregation's designated funds while still practicing mercy toward all. As one guiding principle puts it: we "err on the side of mercy" wherever possible, even when the fund itself is not the right vehicle.
