# Roles & Confidentiality

## Key Roles

### Benevolence Team
One or more deacons appointed to oversee member care, operating under elder oversight (Acts 6:1–7; Bylaws, Article 2, Sections 2b–3b). The team:
- Receives and screens requests
- Conducts pastoral consultations
- Makes decisions (requiring **unanimous agreement**)
- Coordinates disbursements with the Treasurer
- Performs follow-up with recipients
- Reports regularly to the elders on cases and expenditures

### Deacon of Benevolence
The designated lead deacon for this ministry. Serves as the primary point of contact for members seeking help and day-to-day administrator of the fund within the policy's bounds.

### Elder Oversight
Elders provide governance and pastoral guidance. They are consulted when:
- A case is **large, complex, or sensitive** beyond the team's normal discretion
- A request would materially reduce the fund's ability to meet anticipated future needs
- A **second or extended-assistance exception** to the 12-month rule is under consideration (see [[benevolence-12-month-rule|The 12-Month Rule]])
- Special congregational fundraising is needed (see [[benevolence-special-fundraising|Special Fundraising]])

Elders may also deliver pastoral care, counseling referrals, and follow-up alongside the team.

### Treasurer
Records all benevolence disbursements as designated fund expenses. Maintains receipts, confirmation numbers, and the disbursement log. Normal financial controls apply (e.g., dual signatures on checks above certain amounts).

## Confidentiality

All records of benevolence applications and assistance are **strictly confidential**:

- Details of a member's financial situation remain with the Benevolence Team and relevant church leaders only — never shared broadly or treated as general knowledge.
- Even the fact that someone received assistance is not publicized without that person's permission.
- The congregation may be informed generally that the fund is being used ("the benevolence fund helped families this quarter"), but **no names or identifying specifics** are disclosed.
- Donors who contribute to the fund do not direct gifts to specific individuals and do not receive individual reports of who was helped.
- If broader counsel is necessary, information may be shared with an elder or trusted advisor — always in confidence.

Confidentiality honors the Church Covenant's commitment to love, unity, and putting away injurious speech.
