# Eligible Needs

The fund covers **basic living necessities and emergency needs** during a time of crisis. These categories receive priority; the guiding principle is temporary, urgent relief — a "life jacket" in a financial storm.

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Food & groceries** | Grocery expenses or immediate food supply; the church may also provide food directly from the campus pantry / "blessing box." |
| **Essential utilities** | Electric, gas, or water bills to prevent shut-off. Often the *last portion* of the bill is covered, encouraging the member to pay what they can first. |
| **Rent / short-term housing** | A portion of one month's rent to prevent eviction, or emergency lodging (e.g., a motel) in a crisis. |
| **Medical care & prescriptions** | Essential medical bills or medication costs the member cannot otherwise cover. |
| **Job-qualifying education or training** | Tuition or certification required for **legal employment**. Continued assistance requires evidence of satisfactory progress — generally a minimum **GPA of 3.0 or equivalent** — and all training must be lawful. |
| **Other critical expenses** | Evaluated case-by-case; examples include emergency travel for the funeral of an immediate family member or other compassionate grants deemed appropriate by the Benevolence Team. |

> These are illustrative examples, not an exhaustive list. Each request is evaluated on its own merits.

The fund is a **last resort** — applicants should have exhausted personal savings, family support, and insurance before applying. See [[benevolence-who-qualifies|Who Qualifies]] for the full eligibility gates and [[benevolence-whats-not-covered|What's Not Covered]] for ineligible uses.
