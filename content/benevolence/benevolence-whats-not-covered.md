# What's Not Covered

Because the fund is limited and designed for genuine emergencies, the following categories are **ineligible**. The Benevolence Team will decline requests that fall into these areas.

| Ineligible Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Luxury or non-essential utilities** | Cable TV, streaming subscriptions, internet service, cell phone upgrades. |
| **Consumer debt & credit obligations** | Credit card balances, personal loans, payday loan payoffs, business investments. The church will not assume responsibility for accumulated personal debt. |
| **Legal fees, fines, bail, or penalties** | Court costs, traffic fines, bail bonds, or penalties arising from unlawful or irresponsible conduct. |
| **Expenses that enable sin or undermine Christian testimony** | Funding a living arrangement that violates the Church Covenant, subsidizing an addiction, covering gambling debt, or any similar use. |
| **Routine or long-term recurring bills** | Ongoing childcare, elective tuition, or multiple consecutive months of rent. The fund is a short-term bridge, not a sustained subsidy program. |

## Additional Grounds for Denial

- Harassment or disrespect toward church staff or coordinators during the process will result in automatic denial.
- Requests that would **reinforce negative patterns** (e.g., repeatedly covering consequences of reckless behavior or willful idleness) may be declined even if the specific expense type would otherwise qualify.
- In some cases, approval may be made **conditional** on the applicant pursuing financial counseling or another corrective step.

These exclusions reflect the Church Covenant's call to self-controlled, upright living (Titus 2:12) and the goal of truly helping — not enabling — each person.
