# Request Form Fields

The Benevolence Request Form is intentionally brief — it should take only a few minutes to complete. A team member can assist anyone who needs help filling it out. All information is kept confidential.

## Form Fields

- [ ] **Name** — Full legal name of the applicant
- [ ] **Date** — Date the form is submitted
- [ ] **Member since (year)** — Year the applicant joined OIC Church (confirms membership; see [[benevolence-who-qualifies|Who Qualifies]])
- [ ] **Description of need** — A short explanation of what assistance is needed and why (e.g., "Behind on electric bill due to job loss")
- [ ] **Amount requested** — The dollar amount or specific bill requiring payment
- [ ] **Reason / circumstances** — What led to this need? This helps distinguish a one-time crisis from an ongoing pattern and informs the team's evaluation
- [ ] **Steps taken to address the need** — What has already been attempted? (e.g., used savings, asked family per 1 Tim. 5:4, applied for government assistance, contacted insurance)
- [ ] **Assistance requested (type of help)** — Describe the specific form of aid being requested (utility payment, grocery card, rent payment, etc.)
- [ ] **Comments / additional information** — Any other context the applicant wishes to share
- [ ] **Signature and date** — The applicant's signature gives the church permission to verify the information provided and to follow up on the situation

## Required Attachments

- **Copy of the bill or invoice** — Required whenever the request is tied to a specific payable (utility bill, rent statement, medical invoice, etc.)
- **Photo ID** — May be requested at submission to confirm identity and prevent fraud

## Notes

- For larger requests, the team may ask for a brief summary of household income and expenses to understand the shortfall and counsel wisely. Detailed financial statements are not required for small, one-time needs.
- The completed form and any supporting documents are filed securely. Prior requests are tracked to apply the [[benevolence-12-month-rule|12-Month Rule]] consistently.
- Submissions go to the Benevolence Team or church office. See [[benevolence-request-workflow|Request Workflow]] for what happens after submission.
