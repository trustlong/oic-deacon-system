## Overview

The Office of Deacons at OIC is organized into four departments — [[handbook-administration|Administration]], [[handbook-operations|Operations]], [[handbook-finance|Finance]], and [[handbook-communications|Communications]] — each led by a Director and Associate Director, with the whole body overseen by a Chairperson.

## Chairperson

- Elected by the Office of the Deacon (Bylaw 3.3c).
- Duties defined in Bylaw 3.3c include: organizing monthly deacons meetings, organizing congregational members meetings, and keeping records.
- Additionally, the Chairperson consults with elders regularly to assess the church's needs and oversees the overall duties of all departments with necessary guidance and coordination.

## Departmental Leaders and Teams

Each department follows this structure:

| Role | Description |
|---|---|
| **Director** | Primary leader and coordinator for the department |
| **Associate Director** | Secondary leader; assists and covers for the Director |
| **Co-workers** | Ministry team leaders and coordinators |
| **Ministry Teams** | Formed by the Director and Associate Director as needed for short-term or long-term ministry needs |

## General Responsibilities (All Directors and Co-workers)

1. **Identify the needs** — Proactively spot unmet needs in the department's area.
2. **Form ministry teams** — Recruit and organize volunteers and workers to address those needs.
3. **Oversee progress** — Monitor and guide the accomplishment of tasks.
4. **Provide and document policy, procedures, and guidelines** — Maintain written records to ensure continuity.
5. **Report at deacons meetings** — Each department provides regular updates to the full Deacons' Board.

## Handbook Amendments

The Deacons Handbook is a functional document subordinate to the OIC Bylaws. It may be amended by the Deacons' Board to clarify or expand how responsibilities are carried out, subject to Elder oversight. The amendment process requires:

- **Proposal** by any active Deacon.
- **Deacons Approval** — at least 85% of all active Deacons (by meeting vote or documented outside-meeting process).
- **Elders Approval** — unanimous approval of the Elders Board before the amendment takes effect.
- **Recordkeeping** — amendments incorporated into the official Handbook with date and both boards' approval records on file.

Amendments may not assign spiritual authority to Deacons and must remain within the four authorized areas (Operations, Finances, Administration, Communications) as defined in Bylaws Article 2, Section 3b.
