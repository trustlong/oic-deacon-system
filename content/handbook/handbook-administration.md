## Overview

The Department of Administration is led by the **Director (also called the Clerk)** and Associate Director. It covers four functional areas: bookkeeping and records, meeting organization, correspondence, and human resources.

See [[handbook-structure|Office Structure]] for how the department fits within the Office of Deacons overall.

## 1. Bookkeeping and Record Keeping

- Maintain a register of all church members (names, addresses, dates of joining, dates of baptism, and children); add new members to the directory promptly.
- Prepare certificates of baptism and church membership.
- Remove members who have not regularly attended church services for 6 months.
- Assist in writing reports and documents for elders and deacon departments for meetings and records.
- Take minutes at deacons' meetings and congregational meetings.
- Maintain and safeguard repositories of church documents; provide information only to personnel authorized by the elders and/or deacons as appropriate.
- Take record of student services for school credits.

## 2. Meeting Organization

- Call and organize monthly deacons meetings.
- Call for congregational meetings and establish quorums at meetings.

## 3. Correspondence

- Receive, send, and file official correspondence without direction for approval and signature.
- Draft business transactions, contracts, deeds, and titles.
- Prepare information for the Sunday order of service.

## 4. Human Resources

- Oversee the recruiting, interviewing, and hiring process.
- Conduct background checks and employee eligibility verification.
- Oversee employee disciplinary meetings, separations, and investigations.
- Monitor and ensure compliance with federal, state, and local employment laws, regulations, and best practices.
- Oversee all human resource policies, procedures, and implementation for OIC.
- Administer preparation for processing of payroll.
- Work cohesively with the [[handbook-finance|Finance Department]] and church leadership on projects, policies, and procedures.
