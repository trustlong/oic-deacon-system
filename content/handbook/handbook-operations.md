## Overview

The Department of Operations is led by the **Director** and Associate Director. It covers two functional areas: facility management and security/emergency preparedness.

See [[handbook-structure|Office Structure]] for how the department fits within the Office of Deacons overall. Budget oversight for facility-related expenses falls under [[handbook-finance|Finance]].

## 1. Facility Management

- Develop and direct a facility management team at the discretion of church leadership.
- Manage (coordinate and supervise) all work related to maintenance and repair of facilities, including but not limited to: building inspections, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, custodial, landscaping, waste management, and security.
- Ensure all church facilities and grounds are well maintained and ready for Sunday services and mid-week events.
- Manage facility use according to the adopted [[OIC Facility Rental Policy|Facility Rental Policy]], using the [[OIC Facility and Equipment Request Form|Facility and Equipment Request Form]] to intake requests.
- Manage (coordinate and supervise) all janitorial/cleaning of facilities mid-week, weekends, and as needed.
- Purchase and maintain supplies and equipment on an ongoing basis.
- Implement, monitor, and follow policies and procedures to improve operations, minimize operating costs, and provide efficient use of labor and materials.
- Maintain expenditures related to building, facilities, and grounds repairs and maintenance in accordance with the approved budget.
- Work with appropriate government agencies to ensure buildings are safe and meet current code requirements; ensure compliance with OSHA regulations and standards.
- Report problems, solutions, and costs associated with projects not approved in the budget to church leadership; provide input to development of the annual budget.
- Schedule and coordinate work with outside contractors as necessary.

## 2. Security and Emergency Preparedness

- Develop and direct a security team at the discretion of church leadership.
- Develop, implement, monitor, and follow policies and procedures to ensure the security of church facilities and the congregation.
- Ensure church facilities are properly designed and equipped for efficient protection of the congregation in times of natural disasters or hostile attacks.
- Coordinate proper training for the congregation to be ready for emergency situations such as fire, tornado, and hostile attacks.

## 3. Transportation

- The OIC Transportation Service is a volunteer ministry, coordinated under Operations, that provides rides to church-sponsored worship services and activities. See [[OIC Transportation Service - Waiver and Policy|Transportation Service — Waiver and Policy]] for eligibility, scheduling, and the required rider waiver.
