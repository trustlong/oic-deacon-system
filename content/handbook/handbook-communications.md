## Overview

The Department of Communications is led by the **Director** and Associate Director. It covers two functional areas: communication within the church and communication with those outside the church.

See [[handbook-structure|Office Structure]] for how the department fits within the Office of Deacons overall.

## Core Mandate

The handbook is explicit about the department's orienting purpose:

> "Make it easy for people to get the help and support they need from the elders and pastoral staff or church body as a whole."

This mandate connects directly to the diaconal function of [[handbook-what-is-a-deacon|spotting and meeting tangible needs]] and to the member-facing resource at [[need-help|Need Help?]].

## 1. Internal Communication

- Work with church leaders to define, develop, and effectively communicate messages to church-wide and specific audiences.
- Manage the OIC Church brand to ensure consistency, presence, and quality.
- Cultivate, lead, and manage teams of Communications staff and volunteers (writers, photographers, designers, translators, etc.).
- Participate in the creation of the church calendar in advance.
- Consistently monitor and update the church's website and/or social media accounts.
- Keep church members informed of official business, including services, meetings, building or staff changes, and outreach initiatives.
- Establish communication plans for sensitive issues such as crisis communications, staff changes, or deaths.
- Facilitate the church to see and celebrate the work God is doing.
- Educate church leadership and the church body on communication and marketing issues.
- Spur church members to good works by rallying volunteers for church-led ministry initiatives.
- **Make it easy for people to get the help and support they need** from the elders and pastoral staff or church body as a whole.

## 2. External Communication

- Create and implement a system for following up with first-time guests.
- Create campaigns to attract more people to services and events.
- Inform people in the community about services the church offers.
- Study the audiences the church is trying to reach; use that knowledge to recommend and implement outreach strategies.

## Connection to Spiritual Needs Referral

The Communications Department plays a key role in ensuring that when people reach out — whether for tangible help or spiritual care — they are directed to the right person or team. For the distinction between what deacons handle versus what goes to elders, see [[handbook-tangible-vs-spiritual|Tangible vs. Spiritual Needs]]. For the member-facing entry point, see [[need-help|Need Help?]].
