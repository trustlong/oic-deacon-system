Deacons are model servants appointed to a formal church office at OIC (One In Christ) Church. Rooted in Acts 6:1-7, their work encompasses three core functions — spotting and meeting tangible needs, protecting and promoting church unity, and serving the ministry of the elders — carried out across four organizational departments.

This handbook is approved by the Office of Elders and subordinate to the OIC Bylaws. The notes below cover the biblical, theological, and operational foundations of diaconal ministry at OIC.
