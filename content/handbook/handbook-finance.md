## Overview

The Department of Finance is led by the **Director (also called the Treasurer)** and Associate Director. It covers three functional areas: payroll and bookkeeping, budget management, and fund management.

See [[handbook-structure|Office Structure]] for how the department fits within the Office of Deacons overall. The Finance Department works closely with [[handbook-administration|Administration]] on HR/payroll matters.

## 1. Payroll and Bookkeeping

- All finance-related bookkeeping, including checkbooks.
- **Cash revenues (income):** Count, record, and deposit offerings as quickly after services as possible. At least two people must be present when offerings are counted.
- **Cash disbursements (expenses):** See unbudgeted expense approval thresholds below.
- Prepare and submit tax reports and payments in compliance with Federal and State tax regulations.
- Maintain updated records of individual giving and provide annual contribution statements.

### Unbudgeted Expense Approval Thresholds

| Amount | Approval Required |
|---|---|
| Up to **0.5% of the department's approved annual budget** | Treasurer (prior authorization) |
| Cumulative unbudgeted expenses **below 5% of total annual church budget** | Deacons' Board |
| Cumulative unbudgeted expenses **exceeding 5% of total annual church budget** | Congregation |

The Finance Team shall maintain a centralized log of all unbudgeted expenses and provide a **monthly cumulative report** to the Deacons' Board.

## 2. Budget Management

- Draft the annual budget and coordinate with elders and deacons to finalize it.
- Research, evaluate, and sign service contracts within budgeted guidelines (operations such as rental items, internet and phone lines, office equipment maintenance, etc.).
- Assist with preparation and implementation of financial-related policies and procedures.
- Ensure monthly financial statements are available to the elders, deacons, and the congregation.
- Serve as an ex-officio member of meetings related to church budget and financial matters.
- Work with Trustees to ensure appropriate and adequate insurance coverage is in place, including workers' compensation, and property, casualty, and liability insurance.
- Establish and oversee accounting records for church-supported missionary staff, building contracts, or other special projects.

## 3. Fund Management

- Manage designated gift funds, including memorial gifts and deferred giving; assist in managing bequests, endowment, or trust funds.
- **IRS compliance:** The IRS prohibits donors from designating gifts to a specific individual or family for tax-evasion purposes. The church should not serve as a regular channel to pass funds from donors to any individual or for-profit organization. The church has the right to refuse donations with a designated purpose it deems inappropriate or impractical to fulfill.
- Church funds should be deposited in checking or savings accounts to maximize interest income while maintaining access for designated purposes. Church funds **shall not** be used to purchase high-risk bonds, mutual funds, or stocks, or invested in corporations whose mission contradicts Biblical values.

For funds related to the building, see [[building-fund/index|Building Fund]]. For funds related to member assistance, see [[benevolence/index|Benevolence]].
