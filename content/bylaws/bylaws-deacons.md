*Article 2, Section 3*

## Overview

"Deacon" comes from the Greek *diakonos* — "servant." The Office of Deacon is a **separate and distinct office** from that of the [[bylaws-elders|Elder]]. Deacons care for the **temporal needs** of church members and attend to accommodations for public worship, enabling the Elders to devote themselves to prayer and the ministry of the Word (Acts 6:1–6).

> **"Deacons shall not exercise spiritual authority."**

For full operational detail, see the [[handbook/index|Deacons Handbook]].

## Qualifications

See [[handbook-qualifications|Deacon Qualifications]] for the six specific qualities the Handbook draws from 1 Timothy 3:8–12.

- Biblically qualified per 1 Timothy 3:8–12
- Active in all areas of church life
- Minimum **1 year** of church [[bylaws-membership|membership]] prior to serving (exception: initial Deacons)
- Open to **men and women**

The number of Deacons is determined by ministry needs and the call/qualification of members; recommended by Elders and Deacons, approved by the congregation.

## Four Responsibilities

| Department | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **Operations** | Maintain the building and properties; make approved purchases; ensure security of congregation and properties |
| **Finances** | Manage financial assets; secure funds in appropriate institutions; maintain full and accurate accounts of receipts and disbursements; implement financial controls; present financial reports at regular Members' Meetings |
| **Administration** | Manage administrative needs; record minutes of all regular and special Members' Meetings; render reports as requested by the Elders or church |
| **Communications** | Ensure effective communication within the church body and with outside audiences |

## Terms of Office

| Term Length | Consecutive Limit | Sabbatical Required | Extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 years | 2 consecutive terms (4 years max) | At least 1 year after 2nd term | Elders may request congregation allow 1 additional 1-year term in unusual circumstances |

## Organization

The Office of Deacons elects a **chairperson** who:
1. Calls and organizes regular monthly Deacon meetings to discuss ministry issues within the Deacon's duties.
2. Serves as **Moderator** for [[bylaws-meetings|Members' Meetings]], using Robert's Rules of Order.
3. Ensures integrity of meeting records and minutes.

The Deacon chairperson also serves on the [[bylaws-trustees|Board of Trustees]].

## Nomination & Installation

1. A **Deacon Nomination Committee** is formed, consisting of at least one Elder, one Deacon, and elected congregational members; a Deacon chairs the committee.
2. Church members nominate candidates by ballot.
3. Committee evaluates candidates based on 1 Timothy 3:8–13 qualifications and nomination frequency.
4. A committee member meets with each candidate to discuss qualifications and responsibilities.
5. If the candidate senses God's call, he/she notifies the committee.
6. After a season of prayer, confirmed candidates are presented to the church for **voting and ordination**.

See [[bylaws-elections|Elections]] for the annual nomination/election calendar.

## Termination of Office

A Deacon's term may be terminated by resignation or by dismissal if found to be physically or mentally incapacitated or spiritually unqualified (Matthew 18:15–17; 1 Timothy 3:1–7; Titus 1:5–9).
