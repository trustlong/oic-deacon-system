*Article 5, Section 1*

## Fiscal Year

The church's fiscal year runs **January 1 to December 31**.

## Unified Budget Plan

OIC operates under a **unified budget plan**: all undesignated gifts go toward the church's general budget. Designated gifts can only be made to accounts the church has already approved — a donor cannot create a new designated fund unilaterally.

For how the budget is drafted, approved, and reported day to day, see [[handbook-finance|Finance Department]]. For funds related to member assistance and the building, see [[benevolence/index|Benevolence]] and [[building-fund/index|Building Fund]].
