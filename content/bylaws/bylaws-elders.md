*Article 2, Sections 1–2*

## Overview

"Elder" and "pastor" are two different titles for the **same office** (Acts 20:17; Titus 1:5; 1 Peter 5:1–4). OIC is led by a **plurality of elders**. Elders oversee the ministry, resources, and business of the church, subject to the will of the congregation.

No Elder may simultaneously hold the [[bylaws-deacons|Office of Deacon]].

## Qualifications

- Biblically qualified per 1 Timothy 3:1–7, Titus 1:5–9, 1 Peter 5:1–4
- **Men only**
- Minimum **3 years** of church [[bylaws-membership|membership]] prior to serving (exception: initial Elders)
- The majority of active Elders must be **non-paid** (lay) members

## Four Roles

| Role | Scripture | Key Duties |
|---|---|---|
| **Leader** | 1 Tim. 3:4–5 | Oversee deacons and teams; coordinate/promote ministries; communicate to congregation at least quarterly; establish policies; approve facility use guidelines; ex-officio on all standing and task teams (except Pastor Selection Team) |
| **Shepherd** | 1 Pet. 5:1–2 | Follow Christ the Chief Shepherd; protect the flock from false teaching; visit the sick and weak; care for souls |
| **Teacher** | Eph. 4:11; 1 Tim. 3:2 | Preach/teach as needed; ensure all who minister the Word are qualified and sound; ensure worship music is God-honoring |
| **Equipper** | Eph. 4:11; 2 Tim. 2:2 | Equip others for ministry; raise up the next generation of leaders and disciple-makers |

## Types of Elders

| Type | Description | Compensation |
|---|---|---|
| **Vocational Elder (Pastor)** | Full-time, part-time, or regular staff employee of the church | May receive compensation; may not vote on or determine his own salary, benefits, or housing allowance |
| **Lay Elder** | Non-paid member of the church | Not compensated |

### Senior Pastor

The Senior Pastor is an Elder who has devoted his vocational life to the ministry of the Word and prayer. He preaches, administers baptism and the Lord's Supper, and performs related duties. In his absence, the Elders assume his responsibilities (any of which may be delegated).

### Associate Pastor(s)

Associate Pastors are also Elders. They assist the Senior Pastor and may assume his responsibilities during defined absences (sabbatical, illness).

## Terms of Office

| Type | Term | Consecutive Limit | Sabbatical Required | Extension |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vocational Elder | Duration of employment | N/A | N/A | N/A |
| Lay Elder | 3 years | 2 consecutive terms (6 years max) | At least 1 year after 2nd term | Elders may request congregation allow 1 additional 1-year term in unusual circumstances |

## Nomination & Installation

1. Church members nominate candidates to the Elders.
2. Elders evaluate candidates by qualifications (not nomination count); consider one candidate at a time.
3. An Elder meets with the candidate to discuss qualifications and responsibilities.
4. If the candidate senses God's call, he notifies the pastor; Elders discuss and pray.
5. If both candidate and Elders confirm the call, the candidate is presented to the church for an **affirmation vote** and ordination.

See [[bylaws-elections|Elections]] for the annual nomination/election calendar.

### Calling a Senior or Associate Pastor

- Elders nominate; congregation votes at a Special Members' Meeting.
- **90% majority** of voting members present required.
- Quorum for this meeting: **60%** of voting membership.
- The call also receives the pastor (and family) into church membership.

### Pastor Contract Terms

- Annual contract for the first 2 years; 3-year contract beginning in year 3.
- Elders evaluate annually for first 2 years, then triennially.
- Congregation votes on contract renewal.
- Termination: 3 months continued service with normal compensation (unless waived by mutual consent).

### Termination of Pastoral Call

- **By resignation:** 4 weeks' notice to the congregation.
- **By grievance:** Any member may bring a grievance to the Elders. If the Elders find it true and substantial, they call a Special Members' Meeting requiring: (1) Elder recommendation for removal, (2) 60% quorum of voting membership, and (3) **85% majority vote by ballot**. Termination is immediate; compensation rendered within 30 days (minimum 1/12 of annual compensation).

## Meetings & Quorum

- Elders must meet **at least once per month**.
- Quorum: at least **one-half of Lay Elders** plus at least **one-half of Vocational Elders**.
- In matters affecting an individual Elder, that Elder shall recuse himself from discussion and vote at the other Elders' request.
- Elders organize themselves yearly for orderly meetings.

## Decision-Making

All Elder decisions are made after prayerful consideration, seeking scriptural wisdom, voting with humility, and aiming for unity/consensus. Decisions with financial costs should consult the head deacon and treasurer.

Before any item goes to a congregational vote, **Elders and Deacons must reach unanimous agreement** (see [[bylaws-meetings|Members' Meetings]]).
