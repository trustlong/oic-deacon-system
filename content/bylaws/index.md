One In Christ (OIC) is an Elder-led, congregationally governed church affiliated with the Southern Baptist Convention, located in Virginia. These bylaws govern church membership, offices, teams, meetings, elections, and amendment procedures, ratified April 30, 2023 and last amended December 14, 2025.
