*Article 2, Section 4*

## Purpose

The Board of Trustees are the **legal representatives** of One In Christ in the Commonwealth of Virginia, in courts of law, and in business transactions. Their responsibilities include protecting the legal rights and material possessions of the church.

## Composition

| Member | Role |
|---|---|
| Chairperson of the [[bylaws-deacons\|Office of Deacons]] | Ex officio |
| Church Treasurer (from the [[bylaws-deacons\|Office of Deacons]]) | Ex officio |
| 3 active [[bylaws-membership\|church members]] | Elected by the church |

Trustees serve **annually** and hold church property in trust.

## Powers

Upon a specific congregational vote authorizing each action, the Board of Trustees may:

- **Buy, sell, mortgage, lease, or transfer** any church property.
- All legal documents require **at least 3 Trustee signatures**.

## Appointments

Trustees are presented to the congregation by the [[bylaws-teams|Nominating Team]] each May and begin service August 1. See [[bylaws-elections|Elections]] for the full annual cycle.
