*Articles 6–7*

## Amending the Bylaws (Article 6)

Amendments may be made at any [[bylaws-meetings|Members' Meeting]] (regular or special), provided **all** of the following conditions are met:

1. The proposed amendment was **presented in writing** at the previous Members' Meeting.
2. The proposed amendment has the **support of the [[bylaws-elders|Elders]]** before coming to the congregation.
3. Copies of the proposed amendment are **furnished to members no less than 2 weeks** before the meeting at which it is to be considered.
4. The Members' Meeting where the amendment will be considered is **announced from the pulpit on 2 successive Sundays** prior.
5. The amendment passes by an **85% majority** of members present, with at least **60% of the voting membership** present at that meeting. (Unanimity is the goal.)

The revised bylaws shall be made available to all church members.

## Adoption (Article 7)

These Bylaws took effect when **85% of members present** voted to affirm them, no less than 30 days after formal presentation to the church. They supersede and replace all prior bylaws.

A copy is available for all church members to review.
