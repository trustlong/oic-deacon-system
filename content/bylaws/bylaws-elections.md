## Annual Election Cycle

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| **First Sunday of May** | Congregational nominations for new [[bylaws-elders\|Elders]], [[bylaws-deacons\|Deacons]], and team leaders received by ballot; given to the Elders for review |
| **First Sunday of June** | Election for officers by ballot |
| **July 1** | Service begins for newly elected officers |
| **Each May** | [[bylaws-teams\|Nominating Team]] presents slate of servant leadership for the upcoming year (Trustees, Standing Resource Teams, Church Life Teams, and other positions) |
| **August 1** | Service begins for appointed positions (Trustees, teams) |

- Regular elections are held **annually** unless special elections are necessary.
- Ordination for new Elders and new Deacons is scheduled as appropriate and convenient to the church and to the individuals.

## Pastor Contract Renewal (Section 2)

Contract renewal or denial for Pastors (when terms are due) is presented by the [[bylaws-elders|Elders]] and voted on by the congregation at the same June meeting.

## Nominating Team (Section 3 / Article 3, Section 2)

The [[bylaws-teams|Nominating Team]] — 5 members including 1 Elder — is responsible for:

- Discerning the people resources the Lord has given the church.
- Matching gifts to leadership and servant needs.
- Presenting a slate of nominations to the church for approval.

Nominating Team members serve a **1-year term** and are presented to the congregation by the Elders for approval.

## Vacancies

In case of a vacancy in any office (except pastor and staff), the vacancy is filled by the church at a regular or called Members' Meeting as soon as possible after the vacancy occurs (Article 2, Section 6).

## Office Prerequisites

All nominees for church office must:
- Have been [[bylaws-membership|members]] in good and regular standing for at least **1 year** prior to nomination (exception: pastor).
- Receive proper leadership training upon election.
