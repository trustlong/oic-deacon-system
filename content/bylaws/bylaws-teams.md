*Article 3*

Temporary and standing church teams are formed as needed to accomplish the mission and purpose of the church. All teams operate under the oversight of the [[bylaws-elders|Elders]].

## Task Teams (Section 1)

Task Teams are formed to accomplish a specific, defined goal.

- The Elders identify the need, select a team leader from the membership, and ask the leader to pray about serving.
- The leader recruits other team members; additional members may be added at the team's discretion.
- If financial resources are needed, the team develops a budget and presents it to the [[bylaws-deacons|Deacons]].
- The team reports progress to the Elders as needed.
- **Temporary Task Teams** are disbanded once the goal is completed.
- **Standing Team chairs** are elected by the congregation at the recommendation of the Elders at the annual members' meeting.

## Nominating Team (Section 2)

- **Size:** 5 members, including 1 Elder.
- **Purpose:** Match people with gifts to leadership/servant needs; present nominations to the church for approval.
- **Term:** 1 year; presented to the congregation by the Elders for approval.
- Policies and procedures are outlined in the Nominating Team Handbook.
- Meets as needed.

See [[bylaws-elections|Elections]] for how the Nominating Team fits into the annual cycle.

## Church Life Teams (Section 3)

Church Life Teams maintain the ongoing health of church life. The Elders examine, discern the need for, and recommend their creation or dissolution to the congregation. At creation, a description of composition, term, and responsibilities is presented for congregational affirmation.

Teams give a brief report of past actions and future opportunities at each Regular Members' Meeting.

Areas currently identified:

| Team | Focus Area |
|---|---|
| **Discipleship** | Spiritual formation and growth |
| **Outreach/Missions** | Evangelism and global/local missions |
| **Worship** | Corporate worship planning and execution |
| **Counseling** | Pastoral and biblical counseling support |
| **Member Care** | Caring for the needs of church members |
| **Fellowship** | Building community among the congregation |

## Cell Groups (Section 3)

Small groups in which church members are discipled and cared for by group leaders and by one another.

- Group leaders are designated by the Elders at the nomination of the group members.
- Members are encouraged to invite both Christians and non-Christians from the community.
- When a group exceeds a certain size and emerging leaders are present, **the group divides to grow**.
- Operations are detailed in the Cell Group Manual.
