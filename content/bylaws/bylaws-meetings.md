*Article 4*

## Types of Meetings

### Regular Worship Meetings (Section 1)

The church meets corporately **at least on the Lord's Day** and other designated days for worship through preaching, praise, and prayer. The [[bylaws-elders|Elders]] are responsible for the direction of each service.

### Special Worship Meetings (Section 2)

Called by the Elders when there is a special need for prayer, renewal, or a sense of the Lord's stirring toward revival.

### Small-Group / Home Meetings (Section 3)

Cell group, home-based discipleship, evangelism, or prayer meetings may be organized by members throughout the week. These must have **Elder approval** to be considered a church-sponsored activity.

---

## Regular Members' Meetings (Section 4)

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Frequency** | Annual |
| **When** | Second Sunday of December |
| **Purpose** | Prayer and congregational business, including approving the annual budget |
| **Quorum** | 60% of active members must be present |
| **Voting membership** | Active members not currently under [[bylaws-discipline\|church discipline]] |

All members are expected and encouraged to attend.

## Special Members' Meetings (Section 5)

Called for matters of **significant and urgent** nature.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Who may call** | Elders, or written request from 10% of voting membership |
| **Notice required** | Announced at all public services within the **2 weeks** preceding the meeting |
| **Response to member request** | Elders must call the meeting within **1 month** of receiving the written request |
| **Quorum & voting membership** | Same as Regular Members' Meeting (60% quorum) |

---

## How Members' Meetings Are Conducted (Section 6)

Meetings are conducted with decency and order (1 Corinthians 14:40) and with love and selflessness. If a meeting becomes disorderly, the Moderator calls for a time of prayer. **Robert's Rules of Order** may be used at the Moderator's discretion. The [[bylaws-deacons|Deacon chairperson]] serves as Moderator.

### Decision-Making Process

1. Elders hear the accounts of witnesses.
2. Elders find the wisdom Scripture offers.
3. Elders inform the body of the issue.
4. Elders listen to input from the body.
5. Elders formulate a decision.
6. Elders bring the decision to the congregation for affirmation.
7. The body follows the decision of the leadership.
8. The body goes the extra mile seeking a solution.
9. The body seeks unity in the decision.

### Pre-Vote Requirement

> **The [[bylaws-elders|Elders]] and [[bylaws-deacons|Deacons]] must reach unanimous agreement before presenting any item for a congregational vote.** All objections must be thoroughly addressed. If objections remain unresolved, the vote should be postponed to allow further communication.

### Voting Thresholds

| Outcome | Threshold |
|---|---|
| **Decision passes** | 85% affirmative vote of active members present |
| **Matter returned to Elders** | More than 15% of members present do not vote in affirmation |
| **Goal** | Unanimity in all decisions |

- Sensitive matters are voted on using **anonymous ballots**.
- Items not previously known to the Elders that arise unexpectedly during a meeting may be tabled by the Moderator for Elder consideration.
