*Article 1, Sections 1–3*

## Membership Requirements

To be considered for membership, a prospective member must:

1. Express personal faith in Jesus Christ as Lord and Savior, evidenced by a changed life.
2. Have been baptized by immersion after profession of faith, or be willing to be.
3. Attend and complete a **Prospective Member's Class** (covers history, doctrine, governance, SBC affiliation, and service opportunities).
4. Affirm the **Church Covenant**.
5. Meet with at least one [[bylaws-elders|Elder]] to discuss the above.

## Membership Categories

| Category | Description | Voting Rights |
|---|---|---|
| **Active** | Meets qualifications; faithful in worship, ordinances, obedience, service, and financial support | Yes |
| **Watchcare** | Military, college student, or short-term resident who does not wish to transfer membership; afforded all member privileges except voting; membership terminates when they move away | No |
| **Non-Resident** | Moved away for health or family reasons and cannot join a local church in their new area | No |

## Reception into Membership

A candidate is formally submitted by the [[bylaws-elders|Elders]] and voted on at the next appropriate [[bylaws-meetings|Members' Meeting]]. Members may be received by:

- Baptism after public profession of faith
- Transfer of Membership Letter from another Baptist church
- Statement of faith and previous church affiliation
- Restoration after [[bylaws-discipline|church discipline]]

## Member Privileges

- Identified as a true believer under the spiritual protection of godly leaders
- Loving care from the church family
- Opportunity for spiritual growth
- Participation in church business and decision-making

## Member Responsibilities

- Attend worship, prayer services, and [[bylaws-meetings|Members' Meetings]]
- Pray for leadership, one another, and the global church
- Exhibit love for the body; be involved in ministry as gifted
- Provide financial support through tithes and offerings
- Watch over and encourage one another toward holiness

## Dismissal / Loss of Membership

Membership may be terminated by:

1. **Death, transfer, or voluntary resignation.**
2. **Extended absence:** A member who misses all services (worship, Bible study, prayer) for an extended period, and after being contacted continues to be absent for an additional **3 months**, shall be reluctantly released. Rare exceptions for extenuating circumstances.
3. **Church discipline:** A member found guilty and unrepentant after the [[bylaws-discipline|discipline process]] may be dismissed by congregational vote.

> Note: Active membership in good standing is a prerequisite for holding church office (see [[bylaws-elders|Elders]] and [[bylaws-deacons|Deacons]]) and for eligibility in church benevolence and voting.
